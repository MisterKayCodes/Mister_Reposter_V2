# BOT: THE INTAKE CLERK (pairs.py)

### The Character: "The Intake Clerk"
Think of `pairs.py` as the friendly Intake Clerk at the front desk. When you send a message to the bot, she is the one who answers. Her job is to help you fill out the "Reposting Contract" (the RepostPair) without making any mistakes.

---

### The Application Form (CreatePair State)
```python
await state.set_state(CreatePair.waiting_for_source)
```
**Analogy: The Clipboard.**
When you click "Create," the Clerk pulls out a clipboard. She won't let you submit the form until every question is answered. This "Clipboard" (the FSM state) keeps track of where you are in the conversation.

---

### Phase 1 & 2: The Addresses (Source & Destination)
```python
# Create Pair (1/5) - Send the source channel
# Create Pair (2/5) - Send the destination channel
```
**Analogy: The 'From' and 'To' Labels.**
The Clerk asks you for two addresses. She uses the Scout (`resolver.py`) to double-check that the addresses are real. If they are private houses, she asks for a key (the Invite Link).

---

### Phase 3 & 4: The Cleaning Instructions (Filter & Replacement)
```python
# Choose a filter mode:
# Send the replacement link/text:
```
**Analogy: The Laundry Instructions.**
The Clerk asks: "Do you want these messages delivered exactly as they are, or should we wash them (remove links) or even put a new brand on them (replacement link)?" She notes your choice on the form.

---

### Phase 5: The Review (show_preview)
```python
# summary = "🔍 Review Your Pair ... Does this look correct?"
```
**Analogy: The Final Proofread.**
Before she sends the form to the Vault Keeper, the Clerk hands the clipboard back to you. She says, "Is this correct? Once I stamp it, the delivery trucks will start moving!"

---

### Phase 6: The Final Stamp (confirm_pair)
```python
# await repost_service.add_new_pair(...)
```
**Analogy: Dispatching the Order.**
Once you click "Confirm," the Clerk gives the form to the Architect (`repost_service`). He then tells the Scout to start watching the source channel and delivering your messages.

### THE SENIOR FIX: The Lazy Translator
**Analogy: The Multi-Lingual Receptionist.**
When Telegram yells `ChatAdminRequiredError` at the Conductor, the user has no idea what that means. The Intake Clerk (the UI) now works as a translator. She catches that robotic error and shows it on the Stats Dashboard as "**⚠️ I need to be an Admin in the channel!**". She also transforms the standard Pause button into a "**🔄 Clear & Resume**" button, turning a frustrating dead-end into a single-click fix.

---

### Summary for Non-Coders
`pairs.py` is the part of the bot you actually talk to. It's a guided conversation that makes sure the bot knows exactly what you want it to do before it starts the heavy work of reposting.
