# General Career & Tech Ideas

## 1. AI Agents: The New Web
* **Vision**: Static websites are dying. Dynamic AI agents that perform tasks are the future.
* **Focus**: Mastering "Worker" agents (like the Repost Engine) that can run 24/7 without human intervention.
* **Monetization**: Moving from "Selling a site" to "Selling a workforce" (e.g., "I will rent you an AI bot that manages your whole Telegram media empire").

## 2. Remote Networking
* Build a digital footprint while staying indoors.
* Leverage GitHub (Private vs Public) to build a portfolio that proves high-value skills to international clients.
